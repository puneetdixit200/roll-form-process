Direct DWG/DXF import

Files are uploaded to the offline backend, passed through the existing converter and ezdxf reader, reduced to redacted profile candidates, and selected into the existing visual-profile schema. Source CAD is not included in exports.

Interactive editor

Line, polyline, arc, select, pointer drag, delete, undo/redo, pan, zoom, fit, grid/end-point snapping, mirror, rotate, open/close contour, computational seam, coordinate editing, and profile validation are available in the browser.

Stored arcs render as SVG A paths; closed contours require a computational seam.

Generation and evidence

Exact, range and automatic station modes are supported with bounded 8–28 station output. Candidate cards expose style, score, confidence band, mean/minimum pass support, historical coverage and comparison. The pass viewer supports thumbnails, playback, overlay/historical view, top-three matches and metric components.

Exports are generated by the backend: DXF, per-pass DXF files in the ZIP package, SVG, PNG contact sheet, JSON, CSV, HTML and ZIP.

Limitations

  • Only two historical flowers and 31 passes support matching.
  • Visual confidence is not statistically calibrated.
  • DWG conversion depends on an installed offline converter.
  • Closed contours abstain unless compatible closed historical support exists.
  • No physical roller assignment, tooling recommendation, manufacturability claim, or production approval.
